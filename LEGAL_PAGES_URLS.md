# Legal Pages for Razorpay Integration

The following legal pages have been created for your hotel booking system and are ready to be used with Razorpay payment gateway integration.

## Page URLs

### 1. Privacy Policy
- **URL:** `/privacy-policy`
- **Full URL:** `https://yourdomain.com/privacy-policy`
- **Description:** Comprehensive privacy policy covering data collection, usage, sharing, and user rights.

### 2. Terms and Conditions
- **URL:** `/terms-and-conditions`
- **Full URL:** `https://yourdomain.com/terms-and-conditions`
- **Description:** Detailed terms covering booking policies, payment terms, guest responsibilities, and liability limitations.

### 3. Cancellation and Refund Policy
- **URL:** `/cancellation-and-refund`
- **Full URL:** `https://yourdomain.com/cancellation-and-refund`
- **Description:** Clear cancellation and refund policies with different rules for various booking types.

### 4. Shipping and Delivery Policy
- **URL:** `/shipping-and-delivery`
- **Full URL:** `https://yourdomain.com/shipping-and-delivery`
- **Description:** Digital service delivery policy covering instant confirmations, receipts, and any physical delivery terms.

### 5. Contact Us
- **URL:** `/contact-us`
- **Full URL:** `https://yourdomain.com/contact-us`
- **Description:** Contact information, support channels, and a contact form for customer inquiries.

## Razorpay Integration

When setting up your Razorpay account, you can use these URLs in the following fields:

1. **Privacy Policy URL:** `https://yourdomain.com/privacy-policy`
2. **Terms and Conditions URL:** `https://yourdomain.com/terms-and-conditions`
3. **Refund Policy URL:** `https://yourdomain.com/cancellation-and-refund`
4. **Contact Us URL:** `https://yourdomain.com/contact-us`
